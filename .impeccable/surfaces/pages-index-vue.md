## Direction contract

THESIS: ISYF's proof is lineage: 18 Januaries at Hwa Chong's clocktower and the laureates who came. Refuse summit-template kit (eyebrows, badge pills, stat boxes, chip clouds, side-stripe cards) for the school's own heritage objects: facade couplet panels and the honour board.

OWN-WORLD: Clocktower plaster white ground (#f6f5f1 family, cool not cream), couplet crimson (#a3201f) owning whole fields, gilt (#d8b25a) only on crimson, lacquer ink (#1f1a19) for text. Libre Caslon Display headings and engraved-caps names, Lexend body. Hairline rules and double gilt frames, 2px corners, no pills, no shadows.

STORY: Visitor sees the building and name, reads one sentence on what ISYF is, sees the record (editions, laureates), then the honour board of Nobel laureates, then themes year by year, then the 2026 archive and organisers.
